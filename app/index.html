    Why 永?

    永 (yǒng, forever) is the traditional practice character for brush writing. Its five strokes are a dot, a horizontal with a turn and hook, a horizontal that turns into a left-falling sweep, a short left-falling stroke and a right-falling stroke. Together they introduce several shapes you will meet in other characters.

    About this app

    Stroke outlines and stroke order come from the Make Me a Hanzi project, as packaged in the Hanzi Writer character data. They derive from fonts by Arphic Technology and are used here under the Arphic Public License, whose full text is below. The outlines were converted from that data into this app's format on 4 October 2026.

    Stroke names and order follow common practice. Taiwan, Hong Kong and mainland China differ on a few characters, so if your teacher or textbook uses another standard, follow that one.

    Text is set in Noto Serif CJK TC (SIL Open Font License 1.1, Adobe) and Schibsted Grotesk (SIL Open Font License 1.1). The page works offline once it has loaded one time, and your progress stays on this device.
